# 化けの皮 (Bake-no-Kawa)

Hackathon entry, theme **化ける**. You play a fox (化け狐) disguised as a human. The village
"見破り婆", played by Claude, asks you three questions about whatever you disguised as. The more
suspicious your answers, the more the disguise slips: tail, then ears, then fox eyes, and your name
tag turns into real 文字化け (UTF-8 bytes decoded as Shift_JIS).

- Live: https://claude.ai/artifact/9JxJU2CDRoW9YKi5C24uDG (uses the artifact `sample` capability to call Claude)
- Offline: open `index.html` in any browser. With no AI available, a rule-based "オフライン婆" takes over.
